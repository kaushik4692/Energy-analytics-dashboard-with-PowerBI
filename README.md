# Global Energy Consumption & Sustainability Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project is an interactive Power BI dashboard developed using the Our World in Data Energy Dataset. The dashboard analyzes global energy consumption patterns, electricity generation, and renewable energy trends across different countries.

## Objectives

* Analyze global energy consumption trends over time
* Compare electricity generation across countries
* Study renewable energy contributions
* Visualize sustainability-related energy insights

## Tools & Technologies

* Power BI
* CSV Dataset
* Data Visualization
* Data Cleaning & Filtering

## Dashboard Features

* KPI Cards for energy metrics
* Global energy consumption trend analysis
* Renewable energy distribution visualization
* Country-wise electricity generation comparison
* Interactive country filters and slicers

## Key Insights

* Renewable energy generation has increased significantly in recent years.
* Hydropower contributes a major share of renewable electricity generation.
* Energy consumption patterns vary significantly among countries.
* Several countries still show high dependency on fossil-fuel-based electricity generation.

## Dataset Source

Our World in Data – Energy Dataset
